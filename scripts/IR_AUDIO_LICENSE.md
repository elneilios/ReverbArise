# Original IR prototypes and synthetic listening fixtures

The procedural audio produced by `generate_ir_library.py` and the synthetic
listening signals in `tests/ir_library_review.cpp` are original contributions
created for ReverbArise. No recording, third-party IR, commercial effect capture,
voice recording or external sample library is used.

To the extent possible under law, the contributors dedicate these generated
audio assets and listening signals to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They may be copied, modified and redistributed, including commercially.
The source code remains under the repository's applicable source license.

The existing Preverb Hall audio retains its separate documented provenance in
`resources/README.md`. New files under `out/ir-library-prototypes` are prototypes
awaiting listening approval, not locked shipped content.
