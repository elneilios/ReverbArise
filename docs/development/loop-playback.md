# Loop playback scheduler

The Loop route uses the last accepted source identity and exact recorded extent. The next-capture preference controls Learn. The captured grid describes the source, while a verified live phrase marker defines the wet origin and recurring phase. The producer controls the relationship to incoming dry audio through the host's routing. See [Loop host admission](loop-host-admission.md) for the timing and command contracts.

The live adapter checks phase in dry Standby. Cue pins one complete prepared revision and chooses the first future window that can fit the full wet lead-in. The launch forecast intersects the fixed epoch window with the possible intervals from the last observed marker. The forecast stays fixed through the launch, and the wet downbeat follows the observed marker. Perform Stop clears queued work or starts an immediate wet fade of at most 5 ms. If the callback rate changes, the scheduler converts any remaining fade time to the new rate. Loss of sync clears pending work, fades active wet, and requires fresh phase and Cue. Capture commands retain their own roles.

The swell worker reads the same shaped render assets used by Song playback. It publishes and retires Loop plans outside the audio callback and charges the current and held plans against the shared 256 MiB PCM working budget. Preparation checks a bounded range of at most 64 cycle candidates and conservatively bounds simultaneous cycle contributions at 64. The bound includes rounding and lead correction. Exceeding either resource limit makes the prepared plan unavailable and leaves dry audio available. The mixer sums every intersecting contribution from the first scheduled cycle forward.

The shared Perform workspace uses the accepted Loop's native-rate local grid from sample zero through its exclusive end. Source edits must stay within that extent, and wet placement can use signed offsets. The same editor, render and Audition paths serve both source types. Loop edits and finite Audition can prepare while live phase is unavailable. Cue Loop, Cancel Loop and Stop Loop control the recurring wet schedule independently of Audition. The Perform playhead uses the scheduler's phrase position and wraps at the observed phrase boundary. During the first lead-in it follows negative wet time where the view shows it. It clears on stop or loss of sync.

The measured Note Gate route configures the live reference automatically from the observed phrase input. Readiness requires matching rate, tempo, meter and length and two valid matching intervals. Verification begins in Learn after the Loop is accepted and continues into Perform while the same source and live timing remain valid. Learn observes Phrase events without admitting Cue or starting wet playback. A reset, interrupted reference or replacement source requires fresh observations. The route and timing requirements apply independently of dry placement. FL Studio route qualification follows in #127.
